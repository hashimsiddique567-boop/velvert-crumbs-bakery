✦  Our Story

Baked on flour, held together by obsession.

It started with one oven, a family recipe for brown-butter genoise, and a stubborn refusal to use anything from a box. Ten years later Velvet Crumbs still mixes every batch by hand, laminates every croissant at dawn, and tastes every cake before it leaves the kitchen.

✦  How It's Made

From flour
to crumb.

01

Mix

Stone-milled flour, cold water, sea salt — and a starter we've kept alive for nine years. Mixed by hand, never rushed.

02

Proof

Eighteen slow hours in the cold room. Flavour you can't fake, structure you can't shortcut.

03

Bake

Into a scorching stone deck at dawn. Out by seven — blistered, golden, and gone by noon.

✦  Good To Know

Questions we hear daily.

How far ahead should I order a custom cake?+

Standard custom cakes need 5 days' notice. Wedding and multi-tier cakes need 4 weeks — we only take a limited number per weekend so every one gets full attention.

Do you handle allergies and dietary needs?+

We offer nut-free and egg-free options on most cakes, and a gluten-friendly bake on Saturdays. Everything is made in one kitchen, so we can't guarantee zero cross-contact for severe allergies.

Do you deliver?+

Pickup is standard, but we deliver custom and wedding cakes within 25 km — carefully, in a van with the AC on. Delivery is quoted with your order.

When is the bread freshest?+

Loaves come out from 7am and the last bake lands around 11am. Saturday sourdough regularly sells out by noon — call ahead and we'll hold one.

Can I order for a business or event?+

Yes — pastry boxes, dessert tables and weekly office standing orders are our quiet specialty. Email hello@velvetcrumbs.bakery and we'll build a quote same-day.
